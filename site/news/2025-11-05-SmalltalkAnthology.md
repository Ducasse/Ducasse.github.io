Smalltalk is a language that revolutionized programming languages. It was developed at Xerox PARC Palo Alto by the team led by A. Kay and D. Ingalls. I cannot thank them enough for their work and inspiration. I assembled this collection as a tribute to them and all the people who contributed to Smalltalk.

- https://hal.science/SMALLTALK_ANTHOLOGY

As a Pharo visionary, Pharo (https://www.pharo.org) is proud to have such a lineage - we built Pharo on the shoulders of giants. Now Pharo's goal is not to be just a Smalltalk - the goal of Pharo is to bring the idea of Smalltalk and revisit the language infrastructure. This is clearly a large endeavor with the responsibility to honor such a community.

- https://hal.science/PHARO

This collection brings together a set of old books that are no longer in print. These scanned books were donated by the authors/publishers to be included in this list. This collection was created by S. Ducasse and is supported by ESUG http://esug.org. The list was originally hosted at http://stephane.ducasse.free.fr/FreeBook.html. A new version is available at http://stephaneDucasse.github.io/freebooks.html. 

I would like to thank all the authors of the books for their support and for allowing future programmers to learn about the history of a brilliant language. If you have more books for this collection, please contact me at stephane.ducasse@inria.fr
